Infinite Tower Ceiling Calculator

Runs the game's own combat resolution — ported tick-for-tick from CombatSimulator.kt in Idle Fantasy 1.14.0 — over your gear and food, and reports how high you climb. The tower never lets you choose a floor — it always sends you to your current floor + 1 — so this simulates the actual run: you climb until you die, then drop to the checkpoint at (best ÷ 25) × 25.

Reach it is the chance of getting to that floor at all in one run — the product of every clear rate below it — because floors are forced contiguous (QueuedSessionStarter.kt:799). A death sends you back to (best floor ÷ 25) × 25, so a checkpoint needs 25 clears in a row. Best floor is permanent, so a deep run still banks every milestone it passes.
